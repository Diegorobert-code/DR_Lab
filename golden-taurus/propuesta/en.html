Proposal by Diego Robert · October 2026

Everyday life at reception

The same questions, every day

“What’s the Wi-Fi?”

Network, password and a login page that won’t open.

“What time…?”

Breakfast, pools, aquapark, check-out.

“Could you bring me…?”

Towels, a pillow, a taxi, a massage.

With 440 rooms and guests from many countries, every repeated question costs the team time.

The solution

An app on the phone, nothing to download

The guest scans a QR code in the room and the app opens instantly. It works on iPhone and Android, in Spanish, English, German and Chinese.

The first screen solves the most urgent need: getting onto the Wi-Fi.

Possible extension

The lobby model, on the phone

A resort map inside the app: pools, aquapark, restaurants, spa and reception, with the way from the room.

Useful in a 440-room resort, especially on the first day.
